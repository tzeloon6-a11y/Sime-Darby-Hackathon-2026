Household Protocol

Used Cooking Oil (UCO) Storage Guide

Follow these 5 essential steps to prepare high-grade oil for conversion to clean aviation fuel.

warning

CRITICAL SAFETY NOTICE

Hot oil causes severe container warping, toxic vapor release, and severe burns. Always cool completely below 40°C before pouring.

1
thermostat

Allow Oil to Cool Completely (<40°C)

Let pan or fryer rest for at least 60-90 minutes. Never pour hot or warm oil directly into household plastic bottles.

2
filter_alt

Filter Out Leftover Food Particles

Pour oil through a fine kitchen metal mesh sieve or cheesecloth to remove charred food residue. Clean oil maximizes refinery yield.

3
inventory_2

Pour into Clean, Dry Sealed Container

Repurpose clear 1.5L - 5L cooking oil bottles or Sime Darby Circularity jerrycans. Ensure the cap locks tightly to prevent odor.

4
water_drop

Strict Separation: No Water or Liquids

Do not mix with soapy sink dishwater, motor oils, or gravy. Even 2% water contamination degrades kiosk smart sensor readings.

5
local_convenience_store

Drop Off at Sime Darby Smart Kiosk

Take your full bottle to Elmina Central Kiosk #01 or #02. Scan your screen QR to log weight and earn 50 Eco-Points / kg instantly.

You can revisit this guide anytime from your bottom navigation or dashboard.
